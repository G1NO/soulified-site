Your Voice

How to record your vocal at home

You don't need a studio. A quiet room, a phone and headphones get you most of the way. Follow these steps and we can do the rest: tuning, editing, harmonies, mix and master.

What you need

  • A phone, or a USB mic if you have one
  • Wired headphones or earbuds. Bluetooth adds a delay that throws off your timing
  • A second device to play the instrumental, like a laptop or tablet
  • The instrumental of your song. We'll send you one if you don't have it

Pick the right spot

  • Go small and soft. A walk-in closet full of clothes is the best vocal booth most people own. A bedroom with a bed, curtains and a rug works too.
  • Avoid echo. Skip bathrooms, kitchens and empty rooms. Clap once: if you hear a ring after it, find another spot.
  • Kill the noise. Turn off fans, AC and the fridge if you can hear it. Put your phone on airplane mode.

Set up

  • Put the recording phone or mic about 6 to 8 inches from your mouth, roughly a hand's length.
  • Aim it at your mouth, a little off to the side, so hard "p" and "b" sounds don't pop.
  • Use the phone's voice memo app with the highest quality setting. On iPhone, set Voice Memos to Lossless in Settings.
  • Play the instrumental through your headphones only. It shouldn't be in the recording.

Record

  1. Do a quick test line and play it back. If it's distorted, move back a little. If it's too quiet or roomy, move a little closer.
  2. Start every take a few seconds before you sing, and keep the instrumental starting from the top so we can line it up.
  3. Sing the whole song at least 3 times. Different takes give us more to work with.
  4. Then record any parts you want to fix, like a tough chorus line, a few extra times.
  5. Stand up, stay the same distance from the phone, and sing like you mean it. Energy matters more than perfection.

Send it over

  • Share the files through Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer and send us the link.
  • Name them so we can tell them apart: songtitle_take1, songtitle_take2, songtitle_chorus-fix.
  • Don't add effects, autotune or filters. Send them raw. We'll handle all of that.

What to expect

We can tune pitch, tighten timing, stack harmonies from your own voice and mix it into a polished record. What we can't do is change who you are as a singer, and that's the point. It's your voice on your song. If you're unsure, send us one rough take first and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.
