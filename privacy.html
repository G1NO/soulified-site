Privacy

Your info, kept simple

Last updated November 2026.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, email, project details and any links you share, like your song, stems or sample recordings.

How we use it

To review your song or application, reply to you, send quotes, and run your project. We don't sell your information, and we don't add you to marketing lists without asking.

Who sees it

The Soulified team, and, for creators, only the musicians we match to your project, who get just what they need to record. Form submissions are delivered by email through FormSubmit, a form delivery service.

Your music

Songs, stems and recordings you share are used only for your project. We won't share or release them anywhere without your permission.

Your choices

Want your information deleted or have a question? Email us and we'll take care of it.
