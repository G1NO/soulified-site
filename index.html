Rights and ownership

We check before anyone plays a note

Whether you can release your song depends on how it was made. Most services skip this. We don't.

  • We ask which AI platform you used and what plan you were on when you made and downloaded the song, because many platforms only allow commercial use on paid plans.
  • If something looks off, we tell you before you spend a dollar.
  • Musicians record as work-for-hire, so the new recordings belong to you.
  • You get clear written terms on credits and ownership for every project.

Who owns your song?

Short answer: the human parts. A song has two separate copyrights, the recording and the song itself, and AI affects each one differently.

The recording: yours

Every part we deliver is played and sung by real people, and the new recordings belong to you. Human performances are exactly the kind of work copyright protects.

The song itself: it depends

Lyrics you wrote are yours. Melody or lyrics an AI generated from your prompt generally aren't protected, even after a person re-sings them. New parts a person writes, like a rewritten melody or a new guitar line, can add human authorship.

Your Human Contribution Record

Every project comes with a short record of who performed and created what, and when. If you ever register your song, the Copyright Office asks you to explain the human contribution. This is your paper trail.

Where the law stands (US, as of November 2026)

  • AI alone can't own a copyright. On March 2, 2026, the Supreme Court declined to hear Thaler v. Perlmutter, leaving in place the rule that work made entirely by AI can't be copyrighted.
  • Prompts aren't authorship. The U.S. Copyright Office's 2025 report says prompts alone, even detailed ones, don't make you the author of what an AI generates. Human-made parts, real edits and creative arrangement of AI material can be protected.
  • Human contributions get registered. The Copyright Office has registered more than a thousand works that mix AI and human material, protecting the human parts. That includes AI-assisted music.
  • Still being decided: exactly how much human editing or direction is enough. Courts haven't drawn that line yet.

We're not a law firm, and nothing here is legal advice. Copyright law around AI is changing fast. For questions about your specific song, talk to a music attorney.

FAQ

Questions we get

I'm not a musician. Can I still do this?

Yes, that's who we built this for. You don't need to know any music or studio terms. Send the song, tell us what you like about it, and we'll handle every decision from there.

Can I sing on my own song?

Yes. That's our Your Voice package. You record your vocal at home using our recording guide, and we tune, edit and produce it into a finished record. Send a rough take first and we'll tell you honestly what's possible.

Do I need stems?

They help a lot, but they're not required. If you don't have them, send the full track and we'll separate it for the musicians to play along to.

Will it sound exactly like my AI version?

It'll keep your song: the melody, lyrics, structure and vibe. It won't be a copy. Real players bring their own feel, and that's the point. Tell us what you love about the original and we'll protect it.

Who are the musicians?

Every project is produced by Soulified's founder: a producer, guitarist and vocalist with 20 years of experience who has worked alongside some of the world's most established session players and touring musicians, and who plays and sings on many of our projects. We don't believe in name-dropping. We let the music speak for itself. A core crew is already lined up: a drummer, a bass player, male and female vocalists and a second audio engineer. From there we're building a hand-picked roster of top-tier session players, singers and established bands, and we only bring in talent that meets that bar.

Will I own the copyright?

You'll own the new recordings we make. Musicians work on a work-for-hire basis, so the human performances belong to you. The song itself (melody and lyrics) is a separate question: parts you wrote are yours, while parts an AI generated from a prompt generally can't be copyrighted by anyone. Your rights also depend on the AI platform's terms, which is why we check that first. Every project includes a Human Contribution Record documenting who created what. More on ownership.

How long does it take?

Most single parts take a few days. Full band productions take longer. You'll get a timeline with your quote, and rush options are available.

What if I don't like it?

Revisions are included. We'll go back and forth until it feels right, within the scope of your quote.

Can I request a specific artist?

Yes, tell us who. That's what our Signature Artists tier is for. We can't promise any particular artist will say yes, but we'll reach out and let you know what's possible and what it would cost.
